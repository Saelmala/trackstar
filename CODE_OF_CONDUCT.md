# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Trackstar is a small, friendly project. Everyone should be able to take part in
it — asking questions, reporting bugs, or contributing code — without being
harassed or belittled. We are committed to making participation a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level
of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully, and give it kindly.
- Remember that people contribute in their spare time and across time zones and
  languages. Patience costs nothing.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions and commit history — and whenever someone is representing the project
in public.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to
sandra.larsson@eyevinn.se. All reports will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of the reporter will be
respected.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, commits, code, issues and pull requests
that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone
whose behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
